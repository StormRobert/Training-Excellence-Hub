# Seven Stars Brands Ltd: Website Design System

Working name: S2B
Tagline (from the logo file): Learning, Performance and Organizational Development Consultants

## 1. Direction in one paragraph

A corporate site that feels calm and confident instead of heavy. Lots of white, one strong blue doing most of the work, and a small amount of cantaloupe orange to point people at the one thing we want them to do next. Big clear headlines, short paragraphs, generous spacing, real photography, and small moments of motion so the page feels alive without getting loud. The structure borrows from Minty Academy (clear hero, a trusted by strip right under it, grouped programmes, a method section, a closing call to action), the plain service clarity of Brites Management, and the cleanly organized corporate training pages on Simplilearn and Coursera Business.

## 2. What we borrowed from the references

* Minty Academy: a hero with one sentence and two buttons, a stats row right under it, a "Trusted by" logo strip that scrolls slowly, programmes grouped by track, a numbered method section, an FAQ, and a closing band with two audiences. We keep that rhythm.
* Brites Management: very plain service naming, a clients logo row, a short Why Choose Us list, and short testimonials. We keep the plainness. We skip the busy header bar.
* Simplilearn and Coursera Business: team and organization focused pages, a clear "talk to us" path, and programme cards that are easy to scan. We keep the scannable cards.

What we do not copy: dense mega menus, stacked banners, and anything that makes the page feel crowded.

## 3. Colour

Brand colours (given):
* White: #FFFFFF
* Electric Blue: #0297DE
* Cantaloupe Orange: #FC734D

Supporting colours (derived so the three brand colours can be used properly):
* Ink: #0B1B2B (main text, footer background)
* Slate: #4A5B6B (secondary text)
* Blue 700: #0170A8 (blue text, links, small blue elements)
* Blue 900: #03486E (deep blue panels with white text)
* Blue 50: #EAF6FD (soft tinted section backgrounds)
* Mist: #F5F8FA (alternate section background)
* Line: #E1EAF0 (hairline borders)
* Orange 50: #FFF1EC (soft orange tint, used rarely)

Usage ratio on any screen: about 70 percent white and Mist, 20 percent blue (including Blue 50 and Blue 900), 5 percent Ink text, no more than 5 percent orange.

Rules:
* Orange is the action colour. One orange button per section at most. Also used for tiny details such as a number, an underline accent or one icon.
* Electric Blue is the identity colour. Use it for large fills, icons, chips, highlights in headlines, and focus rings.
* Contrast notes. White text on Electric Blue is only about 3.2 to 1, so it is only allowed for large text (24px and up, or 19px bold and up). Small blue text on white must use Blue 700 (about 5.4 to 1). Ink text on Cantaloupe is about 6.5 to 1, so orange buttons always carry Ink text, never white (white on orange is only about 2.7 to 1).
* Body copy on blue panels goes on Blue 900, not on Electric Blue.
* Never place orange text on blue or blue text on orange.

Tailwind style tokens (camelCase on purpose):

```js
colors: {
  ink: "#0B1B2B",
  slate: "#4A5B6B",
  line: "#E1EAF0",
  mist: "#F5F8FA",
  blue: { 50: "#EAF6FD", 500: "#0297DE", 700: "#0170A8", 900: "#03486E" },
  orange: { 50: "#FFF1EC", 500: "#FC734D" }
}
```

## 4. Typography

Both fonts are on Google Fonts. Load only the weights listed.

* Geist for headlines, big numbers and navigation. Weights 500 and 600. Sharp, modern, very minimal.
* Plus Jakarta Sans for body text, buttons, form fields and captions. Weights 400, 500, 600 and 700. Open and friendly, which balances the sharpness of Geist and keeps the corporate tone warm.

Fallback stacks: Geist, then system sans. Plus Jakarta Sans, then system sans.

Scale (desktop first, mobile in brackets):
* Display (hero only): Geist 600, 64 over 68 (40 over 44). Tighten letter spacing by about 2 percent.
* H2: Geist 600, 40 over 46 (30 over 36). Tighten by 1 percent.
* H3: Geist 500, 24 over 32 (20 over 28).
* H4: Plus Jakarta Sans 600, 18 over 26.
* Body large: Plus Jakarta Sans 400, 18 over 30 (used for intro paragraphs).
* Body: Plus Jakarta Sans 400, 16 over 26.
* Small: Plus Jakarta Sans 500, 14 over 22.
* Eyebrow: Plus Jakarta Sans 700, 12 over 16, uppercase, wide letter spacing (about 12 percent), colour Blue 700. Sits above section headlines.
* Big numbers (stats): Geist 600, 56, tabular figures.

Headline trick: one or two words in a headline can be set in Electric Blue to add energy. Never more than two words, never orange.

Line length for paragraphs: 60 to 72 characters.

## 5. Layout and spacing

* Spacing unit: 8px. Use 8, 16, 24, 32, 48, 64, 96 and 120.
* Page container: 1200px wide, 24px side padding (20px on mobile).
* Grid: 12 columns, 24px gutter.
* Section height: 120px top and bottom on desktop, 72px on mobile.
* Alternate section backgrounds between White, Mist and Blue 50 so the page has rhythm without lines.
* Corner radius: 12px for buttons and inputs, 20px for cards, 28px for large image panels.
* Borders: 1px Line. Cards sit on borders first, shadows second.
* Shadow (hover only): soft, wide and low, blue tinted at about 12 percent opacity.
* Breakpoints: 640, 768, 1024, 1280.

## 6. Components

Header
* White, sticky, 72px tall, hairline bottom border that appears after scrolling.
* Logo left. Nav: Programmes, Our Method, Credentials, Clients, About, Contact. Right side: one orange button, "Book a consultation".
* On mobile a simple full screen menu with large links.

Buttons
* Primary: Cantaloupe fill, Ink text, Plus Jakarta Sans 700, 16px, 52px tall, 12px radius. Hover darkens slightly and lifts 2px.
* Secondary: white fill, 1.5px Blue 700 border, Blue 700 text. Hover fills with Blue 50.
* Text link: Blue 700, underline grows from the left on hover, with a small arrow that nudges right.
* Visible focus ring on every control: 3px Electric Blue at 40 percent opacity with a 2px white gap.

Cards
* White, 1px Line border, 20px radius, 32px padding.
* Hover: border turns Electric Blue, card lifts 4px, soft shadow.

Chips
* Small pill, Blue 50 fill, Blue 700 text, 14px. Used for programme groups and durations.

Programme card
* Group chip on top, programme title (H3), one line for who it is for, a duration chip (5 days or 2 days), and a "View programme" text link.
* No fees, no dates and no venues for now. A "Next cohort" line can be added later through the CMS.

Credential card
* A cropped thumbnail of the certificate on a Mist background, the name of the credential, the issuing body, the year. Click opens a lightbox with the full certificate and a close button. Keyboard accessible.

Clients strip
* Full width, white, thin top and bottom lines. A small centred label, "Organizations we have worked with". Logos in greyscale at 60 percent opacity that go to full colour on hover, scrolling slowly sideways. Pauses on hover and stops entirely when reduced motion is on.

CEO block
* One person only. Large portrait on the left (rounded 28px), name, title, a short bio, and three quick facts on the right. No team grid anywhere on the site.

Forms
* Large inputs, 52px tall, 12px radius, Line border, Electric Blue border on focus. Labels sit above the field. Short error text in Ink with an orange icon, never red.

Footer
* Ink background, white text. Logo, short line about the firm, quick links, contact details, and a bottom row for copyright.

## 7. Motion

Keep it small and smooth.
* Sections fade up by 12px over 400ms as they enter the screen, once only.
* Stats count up once when they come into view.
* Cards lift on hover (200ms ease out).
* Hero image has a very slow 2 to 3 percent scale drift, or none.
* Respect the reduced motion setting: switch every animation off and show the final state.

## 8. Imagery and icons

* Real photography of Kenyan professionals in training rooms, workshops and planning sessions. Natural light, candid, not posed stock. No cartoon figures, no clipart globes, no masked illustrations like the old deck.
* Optional soft blue tint overlay on photos so they sit with the palette.
* Icons: a single line icon set (Lucide or Phosphor) at 1.75px stroke, in Blue 700. One orange icon per group at most.
* Logo: use the Seven Stars Brands logo from the deck on white. Request a vector (SVG) version. Keep clear space equal to the height of the star mark. On Ink or Blue 900 backgrounds use a white version.
* Add alt text to every image and a text description for each certificate.

## 14. Voice and copy rules

* Plain, human and direct. Short sentences. Say what we do and who it is for.
* No filler such as "synergy", "leverage", "holistic" or "world class".
* Headlines under 10 words. Paragraphs under 4 lines.
* Draft hero line to test: "Training that helps your team plan, deliver and perform." Supporting line: "Seven Stars Brands runs practical programmes in AI readiness, wellbeing, project delivery, strategic monitoring and customer service."
* Draft closing line: "Tell us about your team and we will suggest the right programme."

## 15. Accessibility and performance

* Meet WCAG AA. Keep the contrast rules from section 3.
* All interactive elements work with a keyboard, with visible focus.
* Minimum tap target 44px.
* Lightbox traps focus and closes with Escape.
* Images in WebP or AVIF with proper sizes and lazy loading below the fold.
* Fonts loaded with swap and only the weights listed.
* Target a fast first load on mobile data, since many visitors will be on phones.
* Set page titles and descriptions for each page and use real headings in order.

## 16. Do and Do not

Do
* Leave lots of white space.
* Let one blue headline word carry the energy.
* Keep one orange action per section.
* Show real certificates and real clients.

Do not
* Fill sections with more than two colours at once.
* Use orange for large backgrounds or for body text.
* Use gradients behind text.
* Show any team member other than the CEO.
* Show any pricing yet.
* Call the utility model a patent.
